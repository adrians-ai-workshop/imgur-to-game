THE CAPTAIN’S GUIDE

How to play

Choose your bet and spin. All 20 lines are active on every spin. Matching symbols pay from left to right, starting on reel one; the highest award on each line counts. Line awards are multiplied by your total bet ÷ 20.

Treasure Hold: Land 6 or more gold coins anywhere to lock them in place. You get 3 respins; each new coin resets the count to 3. Collect all coin values when the respins run out. Fill all 15 spots for an extra 100× total bet!

Coin values: 1×, 2×, 3×, 5×, 10× or 25× total bet. Coins only pay during Treasure Hold. Wins are virtual credits; there are no real-money prizes.
