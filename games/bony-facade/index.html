Halloween dusk. Somebody glued an army of skeletons to a creaky Victorian house with a round turret — and now the bones are on the move. They climb up from the lawn, pick a window, and knock the house down brick by brick.

You are the Jack-o'-Launcher squad. Buy launchers, drop them on the porch roof and turret spots, and blast every climber off the siding before the house hits 0❤.

  • 1 / 2 / 3 choose a launcher (Banger 25· Fizzer 50· Mortar 80) — click a glowing spot to place, click again for Firecracker / Lightning / Cluster upgrades.
  • Click a skeleton (or press Space) to fire a Pumpkin Bomb: 3 bangs per wave.
  • Skeletons pop and fall apart into bone bits and a skull — they fall onto the lawn for bonus bones.
  • Survive 12 waves (moonrise, boss at 12). Esc pause · M mute.
